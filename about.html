Leadership & Scientific Legacy

Mr. Ganesh Shelar

B.Tech. (Food Biotechnology) | MBA (Operations)

With 18+ years of professional experience in Quality Management, Food Safety, Food Science, Process Excellence, Regulatory Compliance and Quality Systems, Mr. Ganesh Shelar brings together scientific expertise, industry experience and a strong passion for Ayurveda and natural wellness.

A B.Tech. in Food Biotechnology and an MBA in Operations, Ganesh Shelar has developed his professional expertise through leading multinational and Indian organizations including Nestlé India Ltd., Mondelez International Ltd., Kellogg's India Ltd., Abbott Nutraceuticals, Mother Dairy, Marico, Privi Organics and Suminter India Organics.

His experience covers diverse areas of the food and wellness ecosystem, including FMCG, food processing, nutraceuticals, beverages, confectionery, bakery, dairy, aseptic processing, frozen foods, IQF products and organic food exports. This extensive exposure has strengthened his understanding of food quality, nutrition, food safety, manufacturing systems and regulatory compliance.
